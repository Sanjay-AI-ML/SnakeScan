# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

SnakeScan is committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors. We are dedicated to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment of any kind
- Discrimination based on any protected characteristic
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Private attacks or doxxing
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing private information without consent
- Spam or self-promotion

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality.

Violations may result in temporary or permanent bans from the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by the SnakeScan project, including GitHub, email, and any official community channels.

Thank you for helping us create a safe, inclusive community. 🐍💚
